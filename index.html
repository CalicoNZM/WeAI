The Science Behind ShadowMap

How solar geometry, urban heat islands, and smart technology converge

The Problem

Cities are overheating. Urban Heat Islands (UHI) can make cities 3-10°C hotter than surrounding areas. Pavement, buildings, and lack of vegetation trap heat — putting pedestrians at risk.

3–10°C Urban heat island intensity

Solar Geometry

ShadowMap calculates the sun's position using solar declination, hour angle, and local latitude. Shadow length = building height / tan(solar elevation).

sin(α) = sin(φ)sin(δ) + cos(φ)cos(δ)cos(ω)

The Solution

Real-time shade prediction using building footprints, tree canopies, and solar calculations. Our AI processes 12,000+ city data points to recommend the coolest routes.
